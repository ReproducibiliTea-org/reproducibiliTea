**The Speaker Swap connects journal clubs looking for speakers for their sessions with potential guest speakers.** 
<br/>
This makes it easier for journal clubs to find external speakers while giving early career researchers the opportunity to speak about their topic(s) of interest.
<br/><br/>
**Are you a journal club organiser looking for speakers for your sessions?** 
<br/>
Send us an email: reproducibilitea@gmail.com and we will send you the link to the list of speakers.

**Would you like to be added to our list of speakers?** 
<br/>
Fill in [our form](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSerVvOK56UqzmnOTgqoRUnWHQO8c_81ofsDbp6P3-XXqkianA/viewform) and we will add you to the list. Journal clubs can now invite you to one of their sessions. Details can be discussed mutually. We particularly encourage early career researchers and students to take this opportunity.

The steering committee is happy to assist whenever needed!
