Advertise your club

  • Add an event to the shared calendar. If your online meeting is open to guests, email us to request access to the shared calendar.
  • Post your club flyer. Adapt a poster template from the organizer toolkit and tag ReproducibiliTea on X (@ReproducibiliT), Bluesky (@reproducibilitea.org), Mastodon (@ReproducibiliTeaGlobal@scicomm.xyz), or LinkedIn (ReproducibiliTea).

Make a meeting card

Download the card to attach it to a post, or choose Share image to open your device’s sharing options when available.
