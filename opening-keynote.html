Opening Keynote · 25 September 2026 · 10:10 – 10:50 UTC+1

Opening Keynote

The day opens with Marta Topor on how community effort — local, national and global — is what turns reproducibility from a principle into everyday practice.

Speakers

10:10 · Keynote · 40 min

Marta Topor

Linköping University, Sweden

Community Efforts Enable Reproducibility

Marta Topor is Assistant Professor at the Disability Research Division and Open Science Coach at Human Open Science Services, Department of Behavioural Science and Learning, Linköping University, Sweden.

Marta works within the field of neurocognitive psychology and has been an advocate for open science since the very beginning of her PhD work. She has worked in the UK, Denmark, and Sweden, engaging in local, national, and global open science community efforts. She is on the managing board of Peer Community In Registered Reports, coordinates a global big team science project and works as an Open Science Coach at her institution. She is passionate about reproducible methods development and enabling inclusive collaborations.
