Journal Clubs · 25 September 2026 · 14:10 – 16:10 UTC+1

Lunch Break & Journal Clubs

A break for tea, then two journal clubs running side by side — one on statistics and responsible interpretation, one on running a ReproducibiliTea journal club of your own. Both start at 15:10, and you are welcome to move between the two rooms as you like — drop in on both if you want to.

15:10 · Journal Club A · 60 min · in parallel

Mircea Zloteanu

StatsTea · King’s College London, UK

Good Science Is More Than Reproducible: Methods, Statistics, and Responsible Interpretation

Mircea is an experimental psychology researcher and educator at King’s College London focused on open science, research methods, and statistics. His work is about making research more transparent, rigorous, and accessible, especially by improving how we design studies, analyse data, and communicate uncertainty.

Mircea also runs StatsTea, where he shares approachable, practical resources on statistics and methods for researchers and students.

15:10 · Journal Club B · 60 min · in parallel

Lianne Wolsink

ReproducibiliTea Steering Committee · Ruhr University Bochum, Germany

Journal club & experience-sharing session: Ten simple rules for running a ReproducibiliTea journal club

Lianne Wolsink obtained a PhD in Psychology and has an academic background in (legal) psychology and cognitive and clinical neuroscience. She has a strong interest in issues relating to research reproducibility, transparency, and integrity.

Lianne started and co-organised the ReproducibiliTea journal club at Ruhr University Bochum, Germany, and is currently serving as chair of the Global ReproducibiliTea Steering Committee. She believes that ReproducibiliTea journal clubs are ideal places for early career researchers to learn, discuss, reflect, and to connect with others, the global community, and other reform initiatives.
