The ReproducibiliTea in Graz, Austria is organised by Gabriela Hofer, Hilmar Brohmer, Ziva Korda, Carmen Pizka, and Eva Boehlke (the Graz Open Science Initiative). 
 
We are a multidisciplinary discussion group but most of our members are psychologists (please drop by to help us change this!).
 
If you are interested to join our ReproducibiliTea please [get in touch](mailto:open.science@uni-graz.at) and come along!
