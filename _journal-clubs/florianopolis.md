ReproducibiliTea@Florianópolis grows out of GEFD's ongoing open sessions on research practice and open science. We're starting this journal club because we want to keep learning about Open Science and Reproducibility together with others who care about it — across disciplines, career stages, and institutions. Hosted at the Federal University of Santa Catarina (UFSC), we're open to anyone in Florianópolis's academic community who'd like to join us, starting April 2027.
