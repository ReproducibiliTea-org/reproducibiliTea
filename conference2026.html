What is ReproducibiliTea?

A grassroots movement for open research

ReproducibiliTea is a journal club initiative that helps researchers create local Open Research clubs at their universities, discussing diverse papers, ideas, and issues around improving research, reproducibility, and the Open Research movement.

Started in 2018 at the University of Oxford, ReproducibiliTea is now completely volunteer-run and offers a unique, supportive community, predominantly for Early Career Researchers.

About this conference

Reflecting on a decade and looking ahead

This one-day online conference marks ten years of momentum behind the push for reproducibility in research. The programme covers the problems the field still faces, how the community has been tackling them, and the grassroots efforts that keep driving change. Researchers, practitioners, and advocates from around the world will come together to share what's been achieved and imagine what the next decade could look like.
